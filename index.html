Stremio for Hisense TV

Click the button below to install Stremio on your Hisense TV (Vidaa OS)

Install Stremio

After clicking, your TV will download and prompt you to install Stremio.

Make sure to allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.

After Installation:

  1. Wait for the download to complete
  2. Follow the on-screen prompts to install
  3. Go back to your TV settings and restore your original DNS settings
  4. Restart your TV
  5. Find Stremio in your app drawer and enjoy!

Recommended: Install APKPure for Easy Updates

Install APKPure app to update Stremio directly from your TV (no PC needed!):

  1. Download APKPure: Click here (if available)
  2. Or download from: apkpure.com on your TV browser
  3. Use APKPure to check for Stremio updates anytime
  4. No computer needed for future updates!

Alternative: Update via PC

  1. Run the update script on your computer: python3 update_apk.py
  2. Start the server again: sudo python3 server.py
  3. Change your TV's DNS back to your computer's IP
  4. Open this page again on your TV
  5. Click "Install Stremio" to update
  6. Restore DNS settings and restart TV
